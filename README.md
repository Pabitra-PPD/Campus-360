🎓 Campus 360

One Campus. One Platform. Endless Possibilities.

Campus 360 is a centralized campus management platform designed to make college life more organized, connected, and accessible. It aims to bring essential campus information, events, announcements, and student resources together in one convenient place.

🚀 Features

* 📢 **Campus Announcements** — Stay updated with important college notices.
* 📅 **Event Management** — Discover and keep track of campus events.
* 🎓 **Student Resources** — Access useful academic and campus information.
* 👥 **Student-Centric Experience** — Simplify access to campus-related services.
* 🌐 **Centralized Platform** — Bring essential campus information together.

> Features will be updated as development progresses.
